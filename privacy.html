Bounce Duel Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Bounce Duel is a game. You can play it as a guest without giving us any information. This page explains what is stored if you create an account.

What we store

  • Your email address and password, if you create an account. They are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. Your password is never stored in plain text.
  • Your game data: the display name you choose, your score, coins, skins, wins and losses, daily challenge progress, and the groups you are in.
  • Settings on your device, such as sound on or off and your chosen skin, spike and power. These stay on your device.

Who can see it

Your display name, score and win counts are shown to other signed-in players in leaderboards and in groups you join. Your email address is never shown to other players.

What we don't do

  • No personalised ads: the ads we show (see below) are family-safe and not based on who you are.
  • No analytics or tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your information.

Where it's stored

Account and game data are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). See Firebase's privacy information.

Ads

The game shows a small banner in the menus (never during a match), and you can choose to watch a short video ad for bonus coins. Ads come from Google (AdMob in the app, AdSense on the website). We ask Google for family-safe, non-personalised ads only and mark them as directed at children, so Google doesn't use them to build a profile of you. Google may still use cookies or device information to show and count the ads; see how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services.

Robot check

When you create an account on the website, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you're a person and not a bot. It looks at basic information from your browser for this and is not used for ads. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Deleting your account

You can delete your Bounce Duel account and all of its data (email, display name, score, coins, skins, stats and group memberships) at any time:

  • In the app: go to the Profile tab, choose Delete account and enter your password.
  • Without the app: open the game in a browser at bounceduel.se, log in on the Profile tab, then choose Delete account.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. Nothing is kept afterwards.

Children

The game does not ask for your age or any personal details beyond an email address for an optional account.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/allo20206real/good-fun-game/issues.
